This is the GT New Horizons modpack, not vanilla progression. Recipes, tool requirements and block harvest levels are modified. Use visible quest-book guidance and NEI recipes rather than assuming vanilla crafting. Early progression involves shelter, dirt, gravel, wood and primitive tools. Hand chopping and vanilla wooden tools may not work as expected. The starting hotbar item is a Quest Book, not an axe. Verify the actually selected item on screen before assuming a tool is equipped.
